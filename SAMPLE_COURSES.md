## Course 1 — POCUS Fundamentals

- **Course Name:** POCUS Fundamentals — Point-of-Care Ultrasound
- **Category:** POCUS
- **Price:** 1500 EGP
- **Duration:** 6 weeks · 24 lectures · 8 hours
- **Passing Score:** 70%
- **Publish Status:** Published

**Short description:**
Learn the essentials of Point-of-Care Ultrasound (POCUS) for emergency and critical-care physicians. Covers probe handling, image acquisition, and interpretation for the most common bedside applications.

**Long description:**
This foundational course prepares clinicians to perform focused ultrasound at the bedside. You'll master probe selection, machine settings, and standard acquisition views for cardiac, lung, abdominal, and vascular POCUS. Every module ends with real case clips and a self-assessment. Designed for Egyptian junior physicians in emergency and internal medicine rotations.

**Instructors:** Dr. Mohamed El-Sayed, Dr. Sara Nabil

**Modules:**
1. **Introduction to Ultrasound Physics** — knobology, artefacts, probe selection *(3 lectures)*
2. **Cardiac POCUS** — parasternal, apical, subxiphoid views · basic function assessment *(6 lectures)*
3. **Lung Ultrasound** — A-lines, B-lines, pleural sliding, consolidation *(4 lectures)*
4. **Abdominal FAST Exam** — RUQ, LUQ, pelvis, pericardial *(5 lectures)*
5. **Vascular Access & IVC Assessment** — landmark technique, ultrasound-guided access *(4 lectures)*
6. **Case Library & Exam** — 10 case clips + final assessment *(2 lectures + exam)*
