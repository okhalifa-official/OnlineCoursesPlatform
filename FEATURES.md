# SonoSchool — Key Features

> A short highlight list — not every capability, just the ones worth calling out on the poster and in the presentation.

## For Students
- **Course catalogue + detail pages** with reviews and ratings
- **Two payment channels** — Kashier card checkout and InstaPay bank transfer
- **AI-reviewed InstaPay payments** — every screenshot arrives with extracted fields and reasons attached
- **Secure PDF viewer** — course materials without downloadable copies
- **Proctored exam engine** — timed, tab-switch detection, clipboard blocked, auto-disqualification
- **Auto-generated certificates** with a public verification code
- **My Courses dashboard** with progress tracking and resume-lecture
- **Payment history** with status pills and one-click retry on rejected payments
- **In-app notifications** for enrollment, payment status, and admin messages
- **Invoice email** on every successful purchase

## For Admins
- **Full course authoring** — modules, lectures, PDFs, exam questions, rich text editing
- **Manage students per course** — enroll, reset exam attempts, issue certificates
- **AI-assisted payment review** — screenshot, extracted JSON, and reasons in one screen
- **Site content editor** — landing hero, tracks, sections, announcements
- **Granular admin permissions** — role matrix (manage users, courses, payments, reports, etc.)
- **System logs & audit trail** — every admin action recorded

## Cross-cutting
- **Role-based JWT auth** for students and admins
- **Public certificate verification portal** — anyone can verify a certificate by code
- **Notification bell** with unread badge and dropdown
- **Consistent design system** — unified navbar + sidebar across every authenticated page
